Vivek Srikumar is an award-winning Ireland-based filmmaker and scriptwriter originally from India.

With a passion for storytelling, Vivek is known for creating thought-provoking narratives that explore identity, suspense, relationships, comedy, and the human condition across diverse genres.

His films have received international recognition through awards, nominations, and festival selections, while continuing to connect with audiences through emotionally resonant and visually engaging storytelling.
